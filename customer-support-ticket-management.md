# Customer Support Ticket Management

This is a sample customer support project showing how I approach customer issues, prioritization, investigation, and resolution.

## Sample Ticket 1: Payment Charged but Booking Not Confirmed

**Issue:**  
A customer was charged for a booking but did not receive a confirmation, and the booking was not showing in the system.

**Priority:** High

**Approach:**
1. Verify the customer's details and payment information.
2. Check the booking system for any pending or failed transaction.
3. Confirm whether the payment was successfully captured.
4. Escalate to the appropriate team if the payment requires further investigation.
5. Clearly explain the situation and next steps to the customer.
6. Follow up until the issue is resolved.

**Customer Experience:**  
Remain calm and reassuring, acknowledge the customer's concern, and avoid promising a timeframe that has not been confirmed.

## Sample Ticket 2: Customer Wants to Change a Destination

**Issue:**  
A customer wants to change their booked destination to a completely different city.

**Priority:** Medium

**Approach:**
1. Review the customer's booking details.
2. Check the ticket's change policy.
3. Confirm whether the requested destination change is allowed.
4. Explain the available options clearly.
5. Provide an alternative where possible.

**Customer Experience:**  
Be polite and understanding while clearly explaining what can and cannot be changed.

---

## My Approach to Customer Support

I believe good customer support starts with listening and understanding the actual problem. I try to provide clear information, avoid making assumptions, and keep the customer updated until the issue is resolved
