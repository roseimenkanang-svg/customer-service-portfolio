# Data Entry & Quality Control

This is a sample project demonstrating my approach to accurate data entry, verification, organization, and quality control.

## Sample Data Entry Workflow

### 1. Receive the Information
Review the source document or information before entering anything into the system.

### 2. Enter the Data
Input customer or business information carefully into the appropriate spreadsheet, database, or CRM.

### 3. Verify the Information
Cross-check important details such as:
- Names
- Dates
- Contact information
- Reference numbers
- Amounts
- Statuses

### 4. Identify Errors
Look for:
- Missing information
- Duplicate records
- Incorrect formatting
- Inconsistent information
- Typing errors

### 5. Correct and Update
Make the necessary corrections and verify them again before finalizing the record.

## Quality Checklist

Before completing a task, I check:

- [ ] All required fields are completed
- [ ] Information matches the original source
- [ ] Duplicate records have been identified
- [ ] Dates and numbers are accurate
- [ ] Formatting is consistent
- [ ] Confidential information is handled appropriately

## My Approach

I understand that even a small data entry mistake can create bigger problems later. My approach is to work carefully, stay organized, and always take a moment to review my work before submitting it
