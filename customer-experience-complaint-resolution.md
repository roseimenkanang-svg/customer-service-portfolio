# Customer Experience & Complaint Resolution

This is a sample project showing how I approach customer complaints, difficult conversations, and service recovery.

## Example: Frustrated Customer

### Situation

A customer was charged for a booking but did not receive a confirmation, and the booking was not showing in the system. The customer was understandably worried because the money had already been deducted from their account.

### My Approach

- I listened to the customer's concern without becoming defensive.
- I checked the available booking information carefully.
- I explained what I knew and avoided giving information I could not confirm.
- I kept the customer reassured while the payment issue was being resolved.
- I remained patient even when the customer contacted us repeatedly.

### Outcome

The payment was reversed in less than an hour, and the customer contacted us again to thank me for my patience and assistance.

## My Customer Service Approach

When dealing with an upset customer, I focus on three things:

1. **Listen** – Understand what is actually frustrating the customer.
2. **Communicate** – Be honest and explain the situation clearly.
3. **Resolve** – Take the appropriate action and follow up where necessary.

I believe a difficult customer interaction can become a positive experience when the customer feels heard, respected, and properly supported.
